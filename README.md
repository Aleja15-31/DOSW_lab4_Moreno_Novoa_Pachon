## Estimation Retrospective

### 1. What was the biggest difficulty when performing the estimation?

The biggest difficulty was that, for some user stories, the estimates were very different between team members. Sometimes, one person suggested a very low value while another suggested a much higher value.

### 2. Was it easy to reach a consensus?

In general, yes. Although we had some differences in our estimates at first, we were able to reach a consensus by listening to each team member's opinion and understanding the reasons behind their estimates.

### 3. How did you resolve situations where the estimates for the same user story did not match?

When the estimates did not match, each team member explained why they had chosen that value. Then, we discussed the different opinions and analyzed whether the user story really required such a high or low effort. Finally, we agreed on a value that reasonably represented the effort required to complete the user story.
